INTENT OF THE REQUIREMENTS (diagnostic condition of this test; stated by the test bench, the quoted sentence is from the NASA publication)
The requirements of this task are formalizations in the sense stated by the NASA authors of the Lockheed Martin challenge formalizations: "Instead of a trigger we wanted the response to hold every time the condition is true and not only when it becomes true from false." (Mavridou, Bourbouh, Giannakopoulou, Pressburger, Hejase, Garoche, Schumann: The Ten Lockheed Martin Cyber-Physical Challenges: Formalized, Analyzed, and Explained, IEEE RE 2020, NTRS 20205000433, requirement AP-003c.)
Where a natural language requirement states a condition, this is the intended meaning of that condition. Which FRETish form expresses it is left to you.
